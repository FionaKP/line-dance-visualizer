The TWINKLE TOES Waltz
Count: 24  Wall: 4  Level: Easy Beginner
Choreo: Val Saari (CAN) - August 2021
Music: The Last Waltz - Marc Hervieux

One EZ TAG & RESTART

Begin on the downbeat following the 18 count intro (just before the lyrics)

BASIC WALTZ STEPS FORWARD & BACK

1-3 Step RF forward, Step LF next to R, Step RF in place

4-6 Step LF back, Step RF next to L, Step LF in place

R TWINKLE, WEAVE R

1-3 Step RF over L, step LF left, step RF together

4-6 Cross LF over R, step RF right, step LF behind R

SIDE/POINT X 2 (R, L 1/2 TURN L)

1-3 Step RF right, Point LF toes L, hold

4-6 Step LF behind R 1/2 turn L (6:00), Point RF toes right, hold

CROSS ROCK X 2, R, L TURN 1/4 LEFT

1-3 Cross RF over L, Recover onto LF, Step RF together

4-6 Cross LF over R, Recover onto RF, Step LF forward turn 1/4 L (3:00)*

REPEAT

*ONE EASY TAG & RESTART: 6 Counts after Wall 4 facing 12:00

STEP/POINT X 2 (FWD POINT L, BACK POINT R)

1-3 Step RF forward, Point LF toes L, hold

4-6 Step LF back, Point RF toes R, hold
