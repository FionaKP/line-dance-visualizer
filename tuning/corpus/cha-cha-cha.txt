Cha Cha Cha
Count: 96  Wall: 2  Level: Intermediate
Choreo: Evon Tanph (MY) - May 2026
Music: Cha Cha Cha - Bruno Mars

1 Restart: Wall 4 after 32Cs

Part A - 64C

S1 Point R, Vine to R

1-4 Point R toe to right, point R toe beside L, point R toe to right, flick R behind left

5-8 Step R to right, step L behind R, step R to right, touch L beside R

S2 Point L, Vine to L

1-4 Point L toe to left, point L toe beside R, point L toe to left, flick L behind right

5-8 Step L to left, step R behind L, step L to left, touch R beside L

S3 R to right, L Recover, R cross shuffle, L to left, R recover, L cross shuffle,

1-2 Rock R to right, L recover

3&4 Step R across left, step L behind R, step R forward

5-6 Rock L left, R recover

7&8 Step L across right, step R behind L, step L forward

S4 R/chair, Shuffle, Back L/R

1-4 Step R forward, L recover, step R back, L recover

5&6 Step R forward, step L behind R, step R forward

7-8 Step L back, touch R back beside L

* Restart here during wall 4 on 2nd time Part A is danced 32Cs

S5 Mambo R/L, Forward Touch/Step R/L

1&2 Step R to right, L recover, Cross R over L

3&4 Step L to left, R recover, Cross L over R

5-6 Touch R toe forward, step R back

7-8 Touch L toe forward, step L back

S6 Dorothy step R/L, R forward, L back, R back, L back besides R

1&2 Step R forward to right diagonal, lock L behind R, step R forward

3&4 Step L forward to left diagonal, lock R behind L, step L forward

5-6 Step R forward, L recover

7-8 Step R back, step L back beside R

S7 Pivot 1/2L paddle

1-4 1/8L turn Step R forward with hips bump (2x)

5-8 1/8L turn Step R forward with hips bump (2x) (6.00)

S8 Hand movement

1-4 Raise both hands up with palm facing up

5-8 Put both hands down with palm facing down until chest level then open both hands to side

Part B - 32C

S1 Basic cha cha

1-2 Step R back, L recover

3&4 Step R forward, step L behind R, step R forward (shuffle)

5-6 Step L forward, R recover

7&8 Step L back, step R back, step L back (shuffle)

S2 Lindy

1-2 Step R back behind L, L recover

3&4 Step R to right, step L beside R, step R to right (chasse),

5-6. Step L back behind R, R recover

7&8 Step L to left, step R beside L, step L to left (chasse)

S3. New York

1--2 Rock R forward across L, L recover

3&4 Step R to right, step L beside R, step R to right (chasse)

5-6 Rock L forward across R, R recover

7&8 Step L to left, step R beside L, step L to left

S4 Walk forward R/L, R forward shuffle, L forward, pivot 1/2R step R forward, L forward, R touch beside L

1-2 Step R forward, step L forward

3&4 Step R forward, step L forward behind R, step R forward

5-6 Step L forward, pivot 1/2R turn step R forward,

7-8 Step L forward, touch R beside L

Endings: Part A after 30Cs, last 2Cs make a 1/2L turn facing to the front with a pose.
