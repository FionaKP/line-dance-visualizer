The basic figures that most line dances are built from. Open a card to watch the boots dance it on a loop — every demo runs at an easy learning tempo, and you can slow it down further with the slider. When a figure clicks, load a real dance in the visualizer and spot it in the wild.

Demos are generated from the figure catalog's canonical counts and played by the visualizer itself — what you see is exactly how these steps render inside a full dance.
